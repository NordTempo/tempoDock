  • Operator is a platform flag on a user, not a workspace role. An operator is usually also an Owner of their own workspace.
  • Operators don't see inside workspaces by default; support access is an explicit, audited switch.
  • Tokens never exceed the person who made them, and can be narrowed to one project or environment.
