Pick

A. Every job stays in view with every column a glance needs (schedule in words and as written, timezone, next, last run, source, on or off) with Disable and Run now on its row, so a failing job is one click from a rerun. The selected job below gets the full width twice: an editor that shows its words and next three times as you type, and the history with the Run tab's open-a-row output, which is the same pattern as the Run tab and the Audit log. B squeezes the list to a few words per job and hides the editor behind a sub tab; C nests three levels of disclosure inside one table and drops the source and timezone columns to fit.
